SRI VIGNESHWARA ELECTRICALS — WEBSITE FILES
===========================================

Website entry page: index.html
Styles: catalog.css
Product gallery and assistant: catalog.js
Product images: products/ folder

The site is bilingual (English and Telugu), works on mobile, and includes the
shop address, daily hours, phone, WhatsApp, product categories, image-led
product details, and a Google Maps directions link. Its catalogue assistant
matches questions to the local product list and suggests relevant items. It
does not call an external AI service. No paid hosting or backend is needed.

PUBLISH FREE WITH GITHUB PAGES
------------------------------
1. Sign in to github.com or create a free account.
2. Create a public repository named: sri-vigneshwara-electricals
3. Upload all files and folders from this outputs directory to the top level
   of that repository. Keep catalog.css, catalog.js, the products/ folder,
   and the logo PNG beside index.html; the page depends on those assets.
4. Open the repository's Settings → Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose “Deploy from a branch”.
6. Select branch “main” and folder “/ (root)”, then Save.
7. After GitHub finishes publishing, the site URL will be shown on that Pages
   screen. It will look like:
   https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/sri-vigneshwara-electricals/

FREE GOOGLE MAPS BUSINESS LISTING
---------------------------------
1. Sign in to the Google account your family wants to manage the shop with.
2. Open https://business.google.com/add and search for the exact shop name.
   If a listing already exists, request access to it instead of adding another.
3. Enter the real shop name, address, phone number, opening hours, and the most
   accurate primary category Google offers (for example, electrical supply
   store). Add only categories and services the shop actually provides.
4. Complete the verification method Google offers. Google decides which methods
   are available; follow the instructions shown in the account.
5. Once verified, add clear photos of the storefront, signboard, interior, and
   products. Keep hours and phone current, and reply to customer reviews.

BEFORE SHARING
--------------
Open index.html on a phone and check the number, address, Telugu wording,
product details, and product availability. Manufacturer images and links are
included as product references; confirm current models and stock with the shop.
The Google Maps button opens a place search for the supplied address. Once the
verified Business Profile has its Google Maps share link, that precise link can
replace the search URL in the two places in index.html that start with
https://maps.google.com/?q=.

The page uses Google Fonts when online, with system-font fallbacks when offline.
